WoW Smart Fishing - credits

Built-in fishing data (Data.lua) is generated from original World of Warcraft 1.12
server data and is distributed under GPL-3.0:

- Zone fishing loot, fishing skill levels and fish school spawns:
  vmangos world database (https://github.com/vmangos), derived from the
  CMaNGOS classic database (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db, GPL-3.0)
- WoW Forever map numbers and zone relationships: QuestieDB
  (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB)
- Classic world map bounds: Questie (https://github.com/Questie/Questie)
