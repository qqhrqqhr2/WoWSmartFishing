# WoW Smart Fishing

Fishing companion for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.



## Features

- **One-button fishing** – one key casts and reels in. Auto lure, auto loot, wider bobber detection with a mouse-over fallback.
- **Session panel** – skill vs. the zone's recommended skill, lure timer, casts, catches, success rate, catches per hour and a *Stop fishing* button that switches back to your weapons.
- **Fishing log** – sessions, recent catches with place and time, a catch journal and a per-zone view.
- **World map**
  - your catch spots, shown with the fish you catch most there
  - fish school spawn points (built-in data)
  - pick a fish to see the zones where it is caught and the chance in each, even on other continents
  - open-water fish list for the zone you are looking at
- **Item tooltips** – where a fish is caught.
- **Sharing** – catches are shared with guild and party members who use the addon.
- **Minimap button** – left-click: equip your best fishing pole (or back to weapons), right-click: settings.
- Six languages: English, 한국어, 繁體中文, Deutsch, Français, Español (auto or chosen in settings).

## Commands

`/sf` log window · `/sf config` settings · `/sf key F` one-button key · `/sf pole` best pole / weapons · `/sf lang en` language · `/sf donate` support link

## Data

Built-in chances and fish school positions come from original Classic 1.12 data (see `CREDITS.txt`). New WoW Forever zones and fish are not included; your own and shared catches fill them in.
