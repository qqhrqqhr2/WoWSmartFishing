# WoW Smart Fishing

## v1.0.0

- One-button fishing: one key casts and reels in (bobber detection with a mouse-over fallback), auto lure and auto loot
- Session panel while a pole is equipped: skill, lure timer, casts, catches, success rate, catches per hour, Stop fishing button
- Fishing log with sessions, recent catches with place and time, catch journal and per-zone view
- World map: your catch spots per fish, fish school spawn points, chances per zone, open-water fish list, fish picker with other continents
- Item tooltips show where a fish is caught
- Share catches with guild and party
- Minimap button: left-click equips your best fishing pole (or switches back to weapons), right-click opens settings
- Settings in Game Settings > AddOns, six languages (English, Korean, Traditional Chinese, German, French, Spanish) with an in-game language option
